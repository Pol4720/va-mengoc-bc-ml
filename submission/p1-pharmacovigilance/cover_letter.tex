\LetterHead
The Editor\\ \emph{Drug Safety}

Dear Editor,

We submit the original research article ``Post-marketing safety of the VA-MENGOC-BC meningococcal B
and~C vaccine in Cuba, \VStudyFirstYear--\VStudyLastYear: a nationwide analysis of spontaneous
reports with disproportionality, latent class and machine-learning methods'' for consideration in
\emph{Drug Safety}.

VA-MENGOC-BC, an aluminium-adsorbed outer membrane vesicle vaccine against serogroups~B and~C, was
the first vaccine shown to be effective against serogroup~B meningococcal disease and has been given
to every Cuban infant for more than three decades. Its post-marketing safety, however, has only been
described in provincial or descriptive reports. We analysed all \PONReports{} deduplicated reports
of adverse events following immunization received by the national surveillance system in
\VStudyFirstYear--\VStudyLastYear, of which \PONTarget{} involved VA-MENGOC-BC.

The study follows the READUS-PV guideline published in \emph{Drug Safety}. Its signal criterion
and four comparator designs---including an infant active comparator, single-vaccine reports and a
masking design that removes the pentavalent whole-cell vaccine---were prespecified, and every event
screened is reported. It separates disproportionality signals for expected reactions from those for
emerging events, and complements them with latent class analysis of co-reported events and
temporally validated models of hospitalisation whose limited value for triage is stated plainly.

The work is also a template for reproducible national pharmacovigilance in settings where
individual data cannot leave the institution: the open-source pipeline harmonises annual files whose
structure changes over time, pseudonymises at ingestion and generates every number in the
manuscript from a public, disclosure-controlled release.

The manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. Both authors
approved the submission. Both are employees of the Finlay Vaccine Institute, the manufacturer of the
vaccine; this is declared in the manuscript, together with \Pending{ethics approval and funding}.
We would be glad to provide any further information.
