\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Release-testing data of every lot were linked to national adverse event reports.
\item Process control and capability analysis assessed the consistency of OMV vaccine lots.
\item A case-only design tested lot attributes against the profile of reported reactions.
\item A negative control, power analysis and lot-grouped validation guarded the inference.
\item Disclosure control kept industrial and personal data confidential.
\end{itemize}
