The Editor\\ \emph{Vaccine}

Dear Editor,

We submit the research article ``Lot release attributes and reactogenicity of an outer membrane
vesicle vaccine: linkage of quality-control data from \PTNLots{} VA-MENGOC-BC lots with national
adverse event reports'' for consideration in \emph{Vaccine}.

Whether lot-to-lot variability within specification affects the reactions reported after
vaccination has been debated since analyses of national reporting systems suggested heterogeneity
between lots of COVID-19 vaccines, and those analyses also showed how easily such comparisons are
confounded. To our knowledge, no study has related the measured release attributes of vaccine lots
to the reactions reported after their administration. Outer membrane vesicle vaccines are a natural
case, because their reactogenicity has plausible lot-level determinants: endotoxin and adsorption to
aluminium hydroxide.

We linked the release record of \PTNLots{} lots of VA-MENGOC-BC produced in
\PTLotFirstYear--\PTLotLastYear{} with the national reports of adverse events following immunization
of \VStudyFirstYear--\VStudyLastYear{} (\PTLinkRate{} of reports linked). The manuscript combines
statistical process control, capability analysis and robust multivariate monitoring of production
with a prespecified case-only analysis of the association between lot attributes and the profile of
reported reactions, including a negative-control exposure, false-discovery-rate control,
sensitivity analyses, minimum detectable effects and a test of incremental predictive value with
lot-grouped validation. Confidential industrial data are protected through disclosure control, and
every number in the manuscript is generated from a public release by an open-source pipeline.

A companion analysis of the national pharmacovigilance data has been submitted separately; the two
manuscripts address different questions and do not overlap in their results. This manuscript has
not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere, and both authors approved the
submission. Both authors are employees of the Finlay Vaccine Institute, the manufacturer of the
vaccine, as declared in the manuscript together with \Pending{ethics approval and funding}.
